How these metrics work

Mean high and population standard deviation summarize the five daily highs. The temperature trend is the ordinary least-squares slope against forecast day; R² describes how closely the highs follow that linear trend. Rain chance is the mean of daily maximum precipitation probabilities.

About the ML forecast

A k-nearest-neighbors analog model learns from eight years of daily temperatures for this location. It matches recent seven-day temperature patterns and season, then predicts the next five daily highs and lows. Walk-forward mean absolute error is compared with a persistence baseline (the latest observed high/low). This experimental baseline is separate from the operational weather forecast.
